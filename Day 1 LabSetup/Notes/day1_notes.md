Cybersecurity & OSINT Lab Journal
Day 1 - Environment Preparation
Date: 04 November 2026

Today marked the start of building my Cybersecurity & OSINT Lab.

The primary objective was to establish a stable and organized environment that can support future cybersecurity learning, OSINT investigations, and practical exercises.

Activities Completed
Installed Kali Linux in Oracle VirtualBox
Verified successful system startup
Updated package repositories
Performed a full system upgrade
Created the initial project structure
Began documenting lab activities
Challenges Encountered
The system update process took longer than expected because a large number of packages required downloading and installation.

Patience was required to allow the update process to complete without interruption.

Lessons Learned
A properly prepared environment provides a solid foundation for all future activities.

Documentation should begin on the first day of a project rather than being added later.

Maintaining records of commands, findings, and screenshots makes it easier to track progress and demonstrate completed work.

Reflection
Day 1 was focused on preparation rather than technical security activities. Although no reconnaissance or investigations were performed, establishing an organized and documented lab environment was an important first step toward developing practical cybersecurity and OSINT skills.
