Reporting & Documentation Report
Date: 07 November 2026

Executive Summary
This engagement focused on understanding cybersecurity reporting, evidence management, and documentation practices.

Scope
The objective was to explore how cybersecurity professionals record activities, document findings, and communicate results through structured reports.

Activities Performed
Reviewed penetration testing reports
Studied report layouts and structures
Examined evidence management concepts
Practiced findings documentation
Developed reporting knowledge
Evidence Collected
Evidence collected during this activity included:

Reporting references
Documentation examples
Notes and observations
Report development exercises
Results
A foundational understanding of professional cybersecurity reporting and documentation processes was established.

Outcome
The engagement reinforced the importance of communication, evidence management, and structured reporting within cybersecurity.

Recommendations
Continue maintaining detailed documentation throughout future lab activities and ensure all findings are supported by relevant evidence.
