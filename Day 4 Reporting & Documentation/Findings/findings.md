Reporting & Documentation Findings
Objective
Develop an understanding of cybersecurity reporting, evidence management, and professional documentation practices.

Finding 1
Cybersecurity reporting is a critical component of any security assessment.

Technical findings must be communicated clearly and accurately to stakeholders through professional reports.

Severity: Informational

Finding 2
Evidence collection supports the validity of findings and conclusions.

Maintaining screenshots, notes, and supporting material helps establish an accurate record of activities performed.

Severity: Informational

Finding 3
Report structure improves readability and consistency.

Common sections such as executive summaries, findings, recommendations, and conclusions help organize information effectively.

Severity: Informational

Finding 4
Documentation should be maintained throughout an engagement.

Recording observations and activities as they occur reduces the likelihood of missing important details.

Severity: Informational

Conclusion
Effective reporting and documentation improve communication, support findings, and contribute to professional cybersecurity practices.
