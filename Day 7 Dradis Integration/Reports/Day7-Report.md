Day 7 Report - Dradis Integration
Executive Summary

The objective of Day 7 was to introduce Dradis Community Edition into the Cybersecurity & OSINT Lab environment and explore its use for assessment documentation, issue tracking, evidence management, and reporting.

Activities Performed

Installed Dradis Community Edition.
Verified package dependencies.
Started Dradis services.
Connected to the local web interface.
Explored project management capabilities.
Created and managed an initial issue.
Reviewed available reporting features.
Results

The deployment was successful and the application became accessible through the browser. Initial issue management functionality was tested successfully. The platform demonstrated the ability to centralize findings and evidence generated during the lab.

Conclusion

Day 7 successfully introduced engagement management concepts through Dradis Community Edition. The platform will be used to organize findings and evidence from future reconnaissance and enumeration activities.
