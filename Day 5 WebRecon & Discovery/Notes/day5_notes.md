Observations
A large amount of information can be collected from public sources before conducting deeper analysis.

HTTP headers and DNS records can provide valuable information about infrastructure and technologies in use.

Lessons Learned
Reconnaissance is an important phase of cybersecurity assessments.

Information gathered during reconnaissance assists analysts in understanding technologies, services, and infrastructure before further investigation.

Accurate documentation remains essential throughout the process.
